Practice areas

Explore all 9 legal practice areas

Legal matters can affect family life, work, property and everyday responsibilities. Review these common areas and contact the office for an initial discussion about your situation.

01 / Family

Family matters

Guidance on divorce, alimony and financial support, child custody, visitation arrangements, and related family disputes.

02 / Civil

Civil disputes

Support with civil claims, compensation requests, debt disputes, and disputes over personal rights and obligations.

03 / Administrative

Administrative law

Questions involving administrative decisions, public services, public employment, and challenges to administrative action where legally available.

04 / Employment

Employment matters

Workplace disputes, employment contracts, unpaid wages, dismissal questions, and employment-related claims.

05 / Property

Property and real estate

Disputes involving ownership, leases, property boundaries, and obligations arising from real-estate transactions.

06 / Contracts

Contracts and obligations

Reviewing contractual obligations, breach-of-contract concerns, and options for resolving disagreements between parties.

07 / Criminal

Criminal proceedings

Information and representation in criminal matters, subject to the lawyer's authorization, experience, and applicable professional rules.

08 / Negotiation

Negotiation and settlement

Exploring lawful settlement options, preparing for negotiations, and helping parties understand possible next steps without promising a result.

09 / Consultation

Initial legal consultation

Discuss the basic facts, identify questions to clarify, review relevant documents, and understand potential procedures and next steps.

How it works

A straightforward first contact

Clear expectations help people prepare for a useful initial conversation. The actual process should reflect the firm's rules and local professional requirements.

Make an inquiry

STEP 02

Confirm suitability

Discuss options

Any advice, fees, engagement terms, and timelines should be agreed directly with the qualified professional.
