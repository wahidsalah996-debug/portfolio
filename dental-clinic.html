Frequently asked questions

A little clarity before you visit

These answers provide a starting point; each clinic should review and adapt them to its own policies.

How do I request an appointment?

Use the clinic's verified phone number or approved booking channel. Your appointment is not confirmed until the clinic replies.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Ask the clinic whether you should bring identification, relevant records, or a list of current medicines. Share health information only through a channel the clinic considers appropriate.

Can this website diagnose a dental problem?

No. This website provides general information only and cannot diagnose conditions or replace an assessment by a qualified dental professional.

What should I do if I have a toothache?

Contact a dental professional to discuss your symptoms and arrange an assessment. Seek urgent medical or dental care if you have facial swelling, fever, difficulty swallowing or breathing, or rapidly worsening pain.
