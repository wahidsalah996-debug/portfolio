# Client Handover & Launch Checklist

This file is for the website owner/developer. It is intentionally separate from the public-facing pages.

## BrightSmile Dental — before launch
- Replace the sample brand name with the clinic's approved name, logo, and visual identity.
- Confirm the actual address, opening hours, phone/WhatsApp contact, and Google Maps destination.
- Replace the placeholder WhatsApp number `000000000000` with the clinic's verified international-format number.
- Confirm that every listed service is actually offered by the clinic.
- Have a qualified dental professional review all clinical wording, FAQs, and treatment descriptions.
- Confirm that the appointment-request form opens the correct WhatsApp destination and that the clinic replies to requests manually; it does not automatically book appointments.
- Review the privacy notice and local requirements for handling patient information. Do not request unnecessary sensitive health details through an unsecured form.
- Test the Arabic, English, and French versions on mobile and desktop, including navigation, links, images, and form validation.

## Northbridge Legal — before launch
- Replace the sample firm name, logo, and brand identity with the approved professional identity.
- Verify the lawyer's credentials, authorized practice areas, jurisdiction, and any professional disclosure requirements.
- Confirm which of the nine listed areas are genuinely offered. Remove any service outside the lawyer's authorization or experience.
- Review family-law wording (divorce, alimony, custody, and visitation) and all other legal descriptions for accuracy under the relevant jurisdiction.
- Replace the placeholder WhatsApp number `000000000000` with the verified professional contact number.
- Update the Google Maps destination with the firm's actual address, or remove the map button if the office address should not be public.
- Add an appropriate privacy notice and secure channel for sensitive case information; advise visitors not to send confidential documents through ordinary chat.
- Check that the site does not promise outcomes or imply that sending an inquiry automatically establishes a lawyer-client relationship.
- Test Arabic, English, and French on mobile and desktop, including navigation, links, and keyboard focus.

## Final quality check
- Open both published pages in a private/incognito browser window and hard-refresh to bypass cached content.
- Check for remaining template language, spelling errors, broken links, missing images, and placeholder contact details.
- Confirm that the published site matches the latest GitHub commit before sharing it with a client.
